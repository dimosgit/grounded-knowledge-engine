# Pilot Location Evidence

## Initial comparison

Valencia offers the strongest balance of operating cost, partner readiness, and a manageable
launch footprint for the first pilot.

Málaga has a strong talent pool but requires more partner onboarding before launch. Lisbon offers
good international connectivity but has the highest estimated setup cost in this comparison.

## Later evidence

The Valencia partner can support the pilot, but the launch window is narrower than first assumed.
The recommendation remains viable if the implementation plan preserves a two-week scheduling
buffer.
